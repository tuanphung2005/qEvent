## Design Specificity Verdict

**Verdict:** Grounded in core domain logic (30s TOTP rotating QR, screen capture protection, tri-color sensory feedback, offline SQLite sync), but compromised by prototype scaffolding and desktop/debug holdovers.

**LLM Assessment:** qCheck demonstrates authentic physical gate understanding, but suffers from Leaked Scaffold Syndrome where quick-login account buttons, sandbox ticket buttons, and emulator test inputs crowd out essential operational tools like flashlight toggles and brightness boosters.

**Code & Detector Scan:** Found 5 explicit zero-border invariant violations (`borderTopWidth={1}` and `borderBottomWidth={1}`), multiple sub-48dp touch targets (back button 32dp, modal close 24dp, sync pill 28dp, Q&A upvote 36dp), and negative shadow offset (`height: -4`) violating the Downward Gravity Rule.

## Priority Issues (P0–P3)

- **[P0] 2.2-Second Scanner Freeze Destroys Gate Throughput:** `ScannerScreen` locks camera and displays blocking full-screen overlay for 2200ms after every scan, capping throughput to ~27 people/minute. Fix: reduce to 600ms, enable tap-to-dismiss, or switch to an ambient HUD border. Suggested command: `/impeccable optimize`.
- **[P1] Remote HTTP Audio Chime Fails in Offline Mode:** `soundService` streams an external Google Actions cartoon sound over HTTP; fails completely in offline mode. Fix: bundle local gate audio assets (`.wav`) using `expo-asset`/`expo-audio`. Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`.
- **[P1] Multiple Invariant Zero-Border Violations:** Explicit 1px borders exist in `login.tsx:173, 206`, `scanner.tsx:229`, `ticket/[id].tsx:206`, and `tickets.tsx:146, 220`. Fix: replace hairline borders with surface contrast and spatial whitespace. Suggested command: `/impeccable distill`.
- **[P1] Sub-48dp Touch Targets on Critical Controls:** Back button is 32×32dp, modal close is 24×24dp, sync pill is 28dp, Q&A upvote is 36dp. Fix: enforce `minHeight: 48` and `minWidth: 48` touch bounds. Suggested command: `/impeccable adapt`.
- **[P2] Leaked Developer Diagnostics Consuming Prime Gate Ergonomics:** Test accounts, IP switchers, and emulator modals crowd out torch toggles and sync queue access. Fix: tuck into hidden debug gestures. Suggested command: `/impeccable distill`.
- **[P3] Accidental Session Logout Hazard on Scanner Header:** Naked logout icon in header without confirmation dialog. Fix: wrap with `Alert.alert` confirmation. Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Alex (Power User / Gate Staff under rush):** 2.2s lockout prevents keeping pace with 300+ arriving attendees; lack of flashlight toggle leaves staff blind in dark venues; logout icon is an accidental tap trap.
- **Jordan (First-time Attendee at noisy entrance):** 30s shrinking countdown induces anxiety that ticket will expire before reaching turnstile; screen capture blocking triggers without onboarding explanation.
- **Casey (Distracted mobile user in bright outdoor sunlight):** Muted Slate 400 (`#94A3B8`) labels fail contrast under sunlight; app lacks auto-brightness booster for QR scanning.

## Minor Observations & Provocative Questions

- `(staff)/sync-status.tsx` is completely unreachable from the UI because tapping the sync badge in `scanner.tsx` opens an alert instead of navigating to the screen.
- Negative shadow offset in `(attendee)/_layout.tsx:20` (`height: -4`) violates the Downward Gravity Rule.
- Question input in Q&A is located in `ListHeaderComponent` and scrolls away rather than being docked at the bottom.
